## Purpose

Use this file to convert raw experience into usable proof for stories, resume, LinkedIn, prep briefs, and interview answers.

## Evidence Sources

Prioritize sources in this order.

1. Performance reviews and manager quotes.
2. Storybank details with concrete results.
3. Debrief notes and transcript highlights.
4. Resume bullets and project summaries.
5. External validation signals such as awards or published outcomes.

## Extraction Fields

For each evidence unit, capture:

1. Event or project label.
2. Candidate ownership statement.
3. Baseline and endpoint metrics.
4. Timeframe and scope.
5. Stakeholders and complexity.
6. Tradeoff or risk handled.
7. Credibility source.

## Evidence Strength Scale

1. Strong: quantified result with clear ownership and timeframe.
2. Medium: clear ownership and impact with partial metrics.
3. Weak: qualitative claim without specific proof.

## Mining Workflow

1. Start with one story or artifact.
2. Extract all candidate owned actions and outcomes.
3. Convert vague claims into measurable statements where possible.
4. Identify missing fields and mark targeted follow-up questions.
5. Save strongest units for high leverage use cases.

## Deployment Map

Use strong evidence first in:

1. resume bullet rewrites.
2. prep question story mapping.
3. concerns counter evidence.
4. pitch differentiator support.

Use medium evidence in:

1. outreach and networking conversation framing.
2. LinkedIn supporting details.

Use weak evidence only as placeholders pending follow-up.

## Evidence Freshness

Mark evidence as stale if older than 24 months unless it represents signature scale or a uniquely differentiated insight.

## State Fields

Track evidence quality in coaching state using:

1. Evidence index freshness: fresh, stale, or not started.
2. Top three strongest evidence units.
3. Critical competency gaps lacking strong evidence.
