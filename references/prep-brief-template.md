## Purpose

Use this template as the standard structure for prep briefs. The prep command remains the command of record.

## Required Inputs

1. Company
2. Role title and level
3. Job description text

## Optional Inputs

1. Interview stage and format
2. Interviewer profiles
3. Candidate constraints or concerns

## Prep Brief Schema

### Role Snapshot

- Company context and role scope
- What success likely means in first 90 days

### Competency Map

- Top 5 to 7 competencies in priority order
- Screening versus differentiating competencies
- Candidate evidence coverage and gap type

### Fit Assessment

- Five dimension fit verdict
- Frameable gaps
- Structural gaps

### Predicted Questions

- 7 to 10 likely questions aligned to top competencies
- One to two high risk question areas

### Story Mapping

- Best fit stories for top questions
- Backup stories and identified gaps

### Concerns and Counters

- Likely interviewer concerns
- Counter evidence and framing strategy

### Questions To Ask Interviewers

- Five tailored questions by stage

### Day Of Plan

- Opening narrative
- Two strongest stories to lead with
- Recovery line for difficult moments

## Notes

Use this schema when manually preparing before running the full prep flow, or to verify prep output quality and completeness.
